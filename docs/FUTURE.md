# Future Roadmap

These features are intentionally not part of v1. Each requires a product and data decision before implementation.

## Candidate features

- AI-generated quizzes: add content, consent, provider, cost, and quality controls.
- Flashcards: introduce prompts/answers and a separate card model.
- OCR/PDF import: parse, preview, deduplicate, and require user confirmation.
- Voice revision: audio permissions, transcription, and privacy policy.
- Calendar integration and exam countdown: external OAuth and event conflict rules.
- Priority subjects and difficulty: new user-controlled signals that affect scheduling.
- Sync/multi-device: authentication, conflict resolution, and tenant isolation.
- Offline/PWA: local queue, encrypted storage, and sync reconciliation.
- Notifications: opt-in delivery, quiet hours, and timezone handling.

The fixed v1 schedule and immutable completion history should remain the compatibility foundation for these additions.
